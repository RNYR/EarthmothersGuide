Earthmother's Guide

A 1-60 Tauren Druid leveling guide for WoW: Forever

Free, and staying free

Earthmother's Guide is an original route from Mulgore to 60, with a built-in arrow. It covers:

  • every druid form quest, from Bear to Cat to Aquatic
  • dungeon quests, a big share of the XP
  • Forever's new zones, such as the Riverglades

It is open source under the GPL, with no paid tier and no locked chapters.

Buy me a coffee

If the guide saved you some alt-tabbing on the way to 60, a coffee helps me keep the route up to date as Forever changes. Tips are optional; the addon itself has no donation link.
